- Page: A4 landscape, 297 × 210 mm.

- Type: Arial Regular, 7 pt throughout; black; no bold or italic text.
- Outlines and lines: pure black, 0.5 pt throughout. Original colored fills are retained.
- Arrows: shaft and filled head form one continuous path object.
- Donut: original colored band retained as a filled annulus with 0.5 pt black outlines.
- Small file-icon lettering is centered inside its original icon. White backgrounds behind the three numeric TPM labels prevent black chart lines from crossing their glyphs. No values or panel positions change.
- PDF embeds Arial. The editable SVG retains live text; the outlined SVG converts letters to paths for copying without font substitution. Both have fixed A4 physical dimensions and a preserved aspect ratio.
- Keep proportional scaling when copying or resizing. At the supplied physical size, fonts are 7 pt and strokes 0.5 pt.
- English and Chinese caption: `figure1-legend.md`; source values: `figure1-source-data.json`.
- Rebuild: install Arial Regular, Inkscape and Pillow, then run `python manuscript/annals/figures/build_reference_style_figure.py` from the repository root. The original `figure1-highlights.svg` must be present. Font files are not redistributed.
