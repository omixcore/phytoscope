Checked 26 October 2026 against the current journal instructions:
https://nyaspubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/17496632/author-guidelines.html

The current Concise Report ceiling is 4,000 words and 50 references. The abstract is one unstructured paragraph, no more than 200 words; up to seven keywords and a graphical summary of at most 70 words are requested. Main text uses Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion and Conclusion. Figure files are separate; panel labels are uppercase. PDF is accepted for line art, with less than 10 MB per figure. Tables are supplied as editable Word files. Required author-controlled declarations include contributions, conflicts and data availability; ethics information applies where appropriate.

This version uses one main figure as requested by the author, also nominated for graphical-abstract reuse. One figure is this manuscript's design choice, not a stated journal limit. Detailed plots are combined as Supporting Figure S1A–D. At the author’s request, Tables S1–S4 are supplied in one Excel workbook rather than separate Word files. The manuscript stays below 4,000 words even when references and the legend are counted.

Contributions, funding, conflicts and archival DOI require final author input. The journal's editorial-permission condition for AI-generated material remains part of its current policy; preparation of this draft does not establish that editorial permission has been granted.
