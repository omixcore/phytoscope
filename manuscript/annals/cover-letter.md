Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, “A portable local framework for gene-structure and expression queries in underrepresented plant species,” as an Original Article.

Researchers working on plants without a dedicated, maintained species query portal may have to combine genome annotations, matching reference sequences, and expression matrices from general public archives. We evaluate whether one local framework can connect these inputs through a recoverable locus-centered workflow. The evidence includes exact source-derived comparison of 4.64 million recognized annotation tuples in four reference systems, selected independent sequence checks in three additional public plant assemblies, and re-quantification of public rice-bean RNA-seq reads against a pinned RefSeq transcript set followed by exact gene-ID reconciliation.

We keep the claims deliberately bounded. The rice-bean reads are from genotype IC426787 whereas the tested reference assembly is cultivar VRB3; the expression exercise therefore validates data import and identifier compatibility, not genotype-matched expression biology. We did not conduct participant research and make no claims of user efficiency, ease of use, or increased discovery. We present the software as a local query framework over user-supplied data, not as a curated species database or a replacement for genome browsers.
